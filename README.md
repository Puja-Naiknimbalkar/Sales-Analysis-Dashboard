# Sales Analytics Dashboard | Power BI 
**Power BI | DAX | Power Query | ETL | Dashboard Development | Business Intelligence | KPI Reporting | Data Analytics**

Overview
This project presents a comprehensive Sales Analysis Dashboard developed in Power BI. The dashboard provides valuable insights into sales performance, product profitability, customer behavior, regional trends, and salesperson performance to support data-driven business decisions.
The objective of this dashboard is to help management and sales teams monitor business performance, identify high-performing products and regions, optimize costs, and improve overall profitability.

Key Features
Interactive and dynamic Power BI dashboard
Sales performance tracking across multiple years
Product-wise profitability analysis
Regional sales distribution and comparison
Customer demographic analysis
Sales representative performance tracking
KPI cards and trend analysis
Drill-down and filtering capabilities
Tools & Technologies Used
Microsoft Power BI
Power Query (ETL)
DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)
Data Modeling
Data Visualization
Microsoft Excel
Dashboard Insights
Sales & Profit Analysis
Total sales and product costs were analyzed across 2014–2016.
Bikes generated the highest revenue and product cost contribution.
Profit trends highlighted opportunities for improving margin optimization.
Product Performance
Road Bikes and Mountain Bikes emerged as top-performing product categories.
Accessories and Clothing contributed comparatively lower revenue.
Regional Analysis
Australia and Southwest regions recorded the highest order quantities.
Sales distribution varied significantly across global regions.
Customer Analysis
Customer segmentation was performed using:
Gender
Education
Occupation
Distance from store
Sales Representative Performance
Sales contribution was analyzed by representative performance.
Austin achieved the highest sales contribution among all representatives.
Business Objective

The dashboard was designed to:

Monitor sales KPIs efficiently
Identify high-performing products and regions
Track profitability trends
Support strategic sales decisions
Improve operational and sales efficiency
Key KPIs
Total Sales
Total Product Cost
Profit
Order Quantity
Customer Count
Regional Sales
Product Performance
Sales Representative Performance
Conclusion

The analysis revealed that Bikes dominate overall sales revenue, while profit margins differ significantly across product categories and regions. The dashboard helps businesses identify performance gaps, optimize pricing strategies, and improve decision-making through interactive visual analytics.
